How does the AI generate cards?

We utilize large language models to scan your PDFs or notes. The AI identifies key definitions, dates, and concepts, automatically formatting them into Question/Answer pairs.

Can I use this offline?

Yes. Once decks are downloaded, you can study anywhere. Using Apple Intelligence models allows for on-device AI processing, so core features work without an internet connection.

What is Spaced Repetition?

It's a learning technique where reviews are spaced out over increasing intervals. LearnHub handles the scheduling so you only study what you're about to forget.

Can I share decks with friends?

Currently under development. We plan to launch sharing and collaboration features in the next few weeks, allowing you to exchange decks with classmates and friends.

Is there a discount for students?

LearnHub is free - all core features are available to everyone at no cost, so there is no paid tier or student discount required.

Which file formats are supported?

PDF and plain text (TXT) are supported today. You can also scan pages with the camera (OCR). DOCX is not currently supported.
